Hello.

Xenon is a quiet, fast browser built on Ladybird. Two questions and you're done, or keep our picks: DuckDuckGo for search, with ads and trackers blocked.

Where do searches go?

Anything you type that isn't a web address goes here.

Private
Mainstream

You can always skip the search engine with a !bang. !w ladybird opens Wikipedia's results and !gh opens GitHub.

Block ads and trackers?

Xenon has uBlock Origin's filter lists built in. They strip ads, trackers, and cookie banners from every site, and nothing is sent anywhere to do it.

Ads
uBlock filters, EasyList, AdGuard
Trackers
EasyPrivacy, uBlock Privacy
Malware and scam sites
URLhaus, uBlock Badware risks
Cookie banners and pop-ups
AdGuard, Fanboy, uBlock

Compact and vertical tab layouts are a right-click away on the tab strip.
