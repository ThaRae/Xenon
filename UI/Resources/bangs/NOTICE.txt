Xenon's !bangs

bangs.json is generated by Meta/update-bangs.py; don't edit it by hand. It holds Kagi's community-maintained bang list, which has most of DuckDuckGo's bangs under the same triggers, plus the few bangs Xenon adds (XENON_BANGS in Meta/update-bangs.py). The list remains under its MIT license, below.

Left out of Kagi's list:
  Kagi searches restricted to one site, which only work on Kagi (170): !4chan, !ada, !adjective, !adv, !aeris, !agdl, ...
  no place in the URL for the query (1): !ass
  queries rewritten by a regular expression, which only Kagi does (3): !ktr, !rsr, !xec
  Kagi's own services, under generic triggers like !help and !api (Helium leaves them out too) (10): !api, !help, !kdic, !kpr, !kt, !orion, ...
